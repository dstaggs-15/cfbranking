    METHODOLOGY

    How the rankings are made

    This is a résumé ranking. The system primarily asks how good a team's résumé is against the schedule it actually played. Strength of schedule is deliberately given a major role.
    01

    Winning Percentage

    Winning matters. A team's overall record is one of the largest components of the final score.

    02

    Strength of Schedule

    SOS1 measures the winning strength of the FBS opponents you actually played. SOS2 measures the strength of those opponents' schedules.

    03

    Scoring Margin

    Winning comfortably matters, but the system caps the influence of scoring margin so ridiculous blowouts cannot dominate the résumé.

    04

    Quality Wins

    Beating teams that are provisionally around the Top 40 earns a small bonus.

    05

    Bad Losses

    Losing to teams around #80 or worse creates a small penalty.

    06

    Location

    Road wins and neutral-site results receive adjustments because not every win or loss happens under the same circumstances.

    07

    Advanced Metrics

    Offensive and defensive PPA plus Success Rate provide a small efficiency check on how teams performed on the field.

    08

    Human Polls

    AP and Coaches rankings can provide a small nudge, but they are deliberately nowhere near strong enough to override the résumé.

    Important: Head-to-head is used as a soft tiebreak when teams are extremely close in composite score. It does not automatically override a substantially stronger résumé.
